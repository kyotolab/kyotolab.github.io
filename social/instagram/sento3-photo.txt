550 yen, a 100°C sauna, no booking. This is how Kyoto locals do sauna.

Skip the hotel spa. Kyoto's neighborhood bathhouses (sento) cap entry at 550 yen, set by Kyoto Prefecture, and some have serious saunas and well-water cold baths.

01 Miyako-yu Shimabara: two cold baths, 18°C and a gentle 20–25°C. 15:00–1:00, closed Wednesdays.
02 Gokou-yu: a sauna room inside a sauna room, 85°C then 110°C. Closed Mondays and the 3rd Tuesday.
03 Sauna no Umeyu: the easiest first sento, by the Takase River. Closed Thursdays.

Wash before you get in, and keep your towel out of the water. Some baths charge extra for towels or soap.
Hours change, so check each bath before you go.
