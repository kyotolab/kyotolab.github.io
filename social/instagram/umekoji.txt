Umekoji Park in Kyoto: a green break near Kyoto Station. Save this for your trip.

Quiet: weekday mornings. Busy: weekends and holidays, late morning to afternoon.
Free: the lawns and paths cost nothing. The Suzaku Garden and the Inochi no Mori forest share one 200 yen ticket (9 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed Mondays).
How to arrive: JR Umekoji-Kyotonishi Station is a 3-minute walk. You can also walk west from Kyoto Station in about 15 to 20 minutes.
With kids: The Kyoto Railway Museum and Kyoto Aquarium are both inside the park, so it doubles as a rainy-day plan.
Autumn: The maples in the Suzaku Garden turn red from about mid-November.
Before you go: check Kyoto City’s free crowd forecast. Search “Kyoto Tourism Comfort Map” (ja.kyoto.travel/comfort).

Hours and prices can change, so check the official information before you go.
