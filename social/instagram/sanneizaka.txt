Legend says that if you fall on this slope in Kyoto, you will die within three years.

Sannenzaka is the stone-stepped lane below Kiyomizu-dera. The legend was most likely a warning to watch your step, and some people carried a gourd as a charm against it. The street is a protected historic district. Next to it, on Ninenzaka, a Starbucks with a noren curtain sits in a townhouse more than 100 years old.

By midday the slope is packed. Go up to Kiyomizu-dera when it opens at 6 a.m., then walk down before 8.
