Shimogamo Shrine in Kyoto: what to know before you go. Save this for your trip.

When: The grounds open at 6:30 a.m. and are free. Mornings are nearly empty.
How to arrive: Get off at Demachiyanagi Station and walk 12 minutes through the Tadasu no Mori forest to the gate. It is the best part, so skip the bus to the door.
Don’t miss: At Kawai Shrine near the forest entrance, the wishing plaques are shaped like hand mirrors. You do the make-up on the face with your own cosmetics.
Snack: Mitarashi dango are said to have been born here. There is a sweet shop just outside the shrine.
Summer: In late July you can wade into the shrine’s pond to pray for good health.

時間: 境内は朝6時30分から、無料。朝はほとんど人がいません。
行き方: 出町柳駅から、糺の森を歩いて楼門まで12分ほど。ここがいちばんの見どころなので、バスで神社の前まで行かずに歩いてください。
見逃さないで: 森の入口近くの河合神社は、絵馬が手鏡の形。描かれた顔に、自分の化粧道具で化粧をして奉納します。
おやつ: みたらし団子はここが発祥とされています。門前に甘味処があります。
夏: 7月下旬は、境内の池に足をつけて無病息災を祈れます。
