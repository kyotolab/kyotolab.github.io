Heian Jingu in Kyoto: what to know before you go. Save this for your trip.

When: The grounds open at 6 a.m. and are free. The square is so wide that it rarely feels crowded, except in cherry blossom season and on 22 October (Jidai Matsuri).
The garden: Shin-en, behind the shrine, opens at 8:30. Entry is 600 yen. Come in early April for the weeping cherries.
Rainy day: The Okazaki area around it has art museums within a few minutes’ walk.
With kids: Kyoto City Zoo is in the same neighbourhood.
Fun fact: It looks ancient, but it was built in 1895. The 24-metre torii came in 1929.
