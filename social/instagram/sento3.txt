3 bathhouse saunas in Kyoto. Entry is 550 yen. Save this for your trip.

Entry to Kyoto's public bathhouses is capped at 550 yen by Kyoto Prefecture, and some have saunas near 100°C and well-water cold baths. No booking, no resort prices.

01 Miyako-yu Shimabara: two cold baths, one cold and one gentle. 15:00–1:00, closed Wednesdays.
02 Gokou-yu: a sauna room inside a sauna room, 85°C then 110°C. Closed Mondays and the 3rd Tuesday.
03 Sauna no Umeyu: the easiest first sento, by the Takase River. Closed Thursdays.

How to: wash before you get in, and keep your towel out of the water. Some baths charge extra for towels or soap.
Hours change, so check each bath before you go.
