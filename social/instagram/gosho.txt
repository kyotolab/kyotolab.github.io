You can walk into the Kyoto Imperial Palace for free, with no booking.

Until 2016 you had to apply in advance for most of the year. Now you simply enter by the Seishomon gate on the west side, after a quick bag check. It is closed on Mondays.

Emperors lived here for about 500 years, from 1331 until the court moved to Tokyo in 1869. The main hall, the Shishinden, looks ancient, but it was rebuilt in 1855, deliberately in the style of the Heian-period palace.

Nijo Castle is about 20 minutes’ walk away, so the two make an easy day on foot and by subway.
