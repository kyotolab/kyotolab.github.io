Fushimi Inari in Kyoto: how to walk the torii gates without the crowd. Save this for your trip.

Quiet: before 7 a.m. Busy: about 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.
Open: 24 hours a day, and free. No ticket, no gate time to wait for.
Tip: The crowd thins the higher you climb. Most people turn back after the first stretch of gates.
Next stop: Tofuku-ji is one station away on the JR Nara line, so the two make an easy morning.
Before you go: check Kyoto City’s free crowd forecast. Search “Kyoto Tourism Comfort Map” (ja.kyoto.travel/comfort).
