Nijo Castle in 4 tips: when to go, what to see first, and where not to take photos. Save this for your trip.

01 Be there at 8:45 when it opens. It is quietest right then, and the last entry is at 4 p.m.
02 Go to the Ninomaru Palace first and the gardens later. The palace is a one-way walk in socks, and tour groups slow it down.
03 Listen for the "nightingale floors". They chirp with every step, but there is no record that they were built as an alarm.
04 No photos inside the palace. It is closed to visitors on Tuesdays in January, July, August and December.

Filmed by the moat at dusk.
Hours and rules change, so check the official information before you go.

二条城の回り方、4つ。
01 8時45分の開城に合わせて。いちばん空いているのは開いてすぐ。入城は16時まで。
02 先に二の丸御殿、庭はあとで。御殿は靴を脱いで一方通行。団体が入ると進みにくくなります。
03 鶯張りの廊下は、歩くたびにキュッキュッと鳴ります。防犯のために作ったという記録はありません。
04 御殿の中は撮影できません。1月・7月・8月・12月の毎週火曜は、御殿に入れません。
営業時間や規則は変わるので、行く前に公式情報をご確認ください。
