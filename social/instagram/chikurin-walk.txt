POV: walking into the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove in Kyoto. It is free and always open, and quietest before 8 a.m. Save this for your trip.

Quiet: before 8 a.m. Busy: after 8, when the path turns into a slow line.
Open: always, and free. There is no gate and no ticket.
Next stop: walk on to the Togetsukyo Bridge. The two make a half day.
Before you go: check Kyoto City’s free crowd forecast. Search “Kyoto Tourism Comfort Map” (ja.kyoto.travel/comfort).
