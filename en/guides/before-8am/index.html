Kyoto Before 8AM

FOUR MORNING ROUTES

Kyoto is two different cities, and the quiet one is over by breakfast.

Why mornings

After 8am the Bamboo Grove fills and you move with the flow of people. At 11 the stage at Kiyomizu-dera holds hundreds; at 6, when the temple opens, it holds fewer than ten. The same places, at a different hour, are different places.

Our free spot guides tell you when to go to each sight. This guide puts them together: four half-day routes, each planned from an early start to a late breakfast.

The four routes

  1. ROUTE 1

    Arashiyama

    The bamboo grove at 7, then the bridge from the far bank. Arashiyama fits into half a day.

    Bamboo Grove → Togetsukyo

  2. ROUTE 2

    Higashiyama

    Kiyomizu-dera at its 6am opening, then downhill through the slopes before the shops open, and on to Gion.

    Kiyomizu-dera → Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka → Gion

  3. ROUTE 3

    The south

    The torii tunnel at dawn, then one stop on the JR Nara line. Both southern sights before lunch.

    Fushimi Inari → Tofuku-ji

  4. ROUTE 4

    The north-east

    Ginkaku-ji first, then south along the canal to Nanzen-ji. A half day with almost no buses.

    Ginkaku-ji → Philosopher’s Path → Nanzen-ji
