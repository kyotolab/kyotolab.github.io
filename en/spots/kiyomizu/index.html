Spot guide 02

Kiyomizu-dera

KIYOMIZU-DERA

Of Kyoto’s major temples, it is the only one that opens at 6am.

When to go

It opens at 6am all year round. No other major temple in Kyoto opens this early.

There is a window when fewer than ten people stand on the stage, and the city lies below in the morning haze. Come at 11 and the same stage holds hundreds. At 6 and at 11, it is two different temples.

What to look at

The stage, and the wooden pillars that hold it up from below. Look at the city from the top of the stage, and at how the pillars are joined from underneath it.

On the way back, walk down Kiyomizu-zaka to Sannen-zaka and Ninen-zaka. At 7 the shops are still shut and you see the stone lanes themselves. It is also the only hour when you can look up at the Yasaka pagoda from the bottom of the slope with nobody in the frame.

Watch out for

Climbing Kiyomizu-zaka with a large suitcase is not realistic. In the busy seasons the lockers at Kyoto Station are full by late morning. Book the station’s baggage storage, or a hotel-to-hotel luggage delivery, before you go.
