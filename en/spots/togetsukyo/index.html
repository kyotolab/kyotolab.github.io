Spot guide 04

Togetsukyo

TOGETSUKYO

The bridge is quicker to look at than to cross.

When to go

In the autumn leaf season the bridge becomes a one-way column of people. The busier the season, the less reason there is to stay on the bridge.

What to look at

From the Nakanoshima side, across the river, you get the bridge and the hills in one view. From on the bridge you cannot see the bridge. Look at the Arashiyama hills in colour, the Katsura River below them, and the bridge together.

How to fit it in

It is in Arashiyama, like the bamboo grove. Walk the bamboo grove in the morning, then come to the bridge, and Arashiyama fits into half a day. Arashiyama lies in the opposite direction from the city centre, so we do not recommend adding it to a day of sights in other wards.
