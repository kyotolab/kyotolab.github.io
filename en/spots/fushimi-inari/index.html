Spot guide 03

Fushimi Inari

FUSHIMI INARI

It is open 24 hours, so there is no reason to queue at midday.

When to go

You can visit at any hour of the day or night, and there is no admission fee. So the torii tunnel is not somewhere to queue for at midday; it is somewhere to walk at night or first thing in the morning.

If you walk it at night, bring a light for your feet.

What to look at

The Senbon Torii, thousands of vermilion gates in rows. The vermilion shows best in low light. Walk it when nobody is there and you see the gates run on into the distance.

How to fit it in

Fushimi Inari is in Fushimi ward, away from the centre (Higashiyama, Nakagyo and Shimogyo). Rather than squeezing it into a day of central sights, give it an early morning or an evening on a different day.
